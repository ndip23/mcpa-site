About MCPA

Who we are and what drives us

Who We Are

Motivating Change for Progress in Africa (MCPA) is a non-profit organisation headquartered in Buea, Cameroon. We work at the intersection of education, advocacy, and community development to create lasting positive change for youth and communities across Africa.

Founded on the belief that behavioral change is the foundation of sustainable development, MCPA designs programs that equip individuals with the knowledge, skills, and values needed to shape a better future.
